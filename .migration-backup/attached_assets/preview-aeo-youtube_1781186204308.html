AEO — LLM Visibility

AI Engine Optimisation

When someone asks an AI model about Indian air quality, which organisations does it cite? Five standard discovery questions were sent to three LLMs. Mention rate = AEO score. Contributes 30% of the Competitive Scorecard.
How AEO is measured: Each LLM (GPT-4o mini, Perplexity Sonar, Gemini 1.5 Flash) received 5 standard AQ discovery questions. Each response that names the org = 1 mention. Score = (total mentions ÷ 15 possible) × 100.
